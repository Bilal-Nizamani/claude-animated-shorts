# "The Cook Who Survived 3 Days Underwater" - script v1 (serious tone, no jokes)
Status: WAITING FOR APPROVAL. ~226 spoken words, ~61 s at 3.7 words/s. Spoken text lives in narration.txt.

## Facts and sources
- 26 May 2013: tugboat Jascon-4 capsized in heavy swell off Nigeria (Gulf of Guinea), ~20 miles out, and came to rest upside down on the seabed at ~30 m (100 ft). [Wikipedia: Harrison Odjegba Okene; NBC News; Fox News]
- 12 aboard (11 Nigerians + a Ukrainian captain). Okene, the 29-year-old ship's cook, was the only survivor; 11 died. [Fox News; NBC News]
- He felt his way through the dark wreck to a ~1.2 m (4 ft) air pocket (engineers' office / toilet and bedroom area). He stayed afloat on two mattresses. [Wikipedia; Fox News; NBC News]
- Cold water, total darkness, water rising from the ceiling and shrinking the bubble. He prayed repeatedly. [NBC News; Fox News]
- Found by South African saturation divers searching for bodies; he touched / waved at the diver, who was shocked. ~60+ hours after the capsize ("nearly three days" in several outlets). [Fox News; NBC News; Wikipedia]
- Physicist estimate: the air compressed by water pressure could sustain him ~2.5 days (60 h). [reported via Fox News / Lawrence Livermore estimate]
- Decompression chamber afterwards, about 2.5 days. [Wikipedia; NBC News]
- He later became a commercial diver (IMCA Class 2 certificate). [Wikipedia]
